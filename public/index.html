Meet your stylist

Hi, I’m Valery.

I’m a Miami-trained hairstylist with 13 years of experience, proudly serving San Antonio since 2017. I specialize in balayage, dimensional color, highlights, root touch-ups, and precision haircuts for women, men and children.

With a background in blow-dry bar styling, creating beautiful, long-lasting blowouts is one of my favorite parts of every appointment.

Good to know

Before your visit

A few details that help every appointment feel relaxed and on time.

How do I book?

Book online anytime through Vagaro, or call (726) 231-6067. A valid credit card is required to reserve your appointment.

How should I prepare?
  • Please arrive 5–10 minutes early.
  • Come with clean, dry hair unless your service includes a shampoo.
  • Let me know about any box color, extensions or recent chemical services.
  • Inspiration photos are always welcome!
  • To keep the space relaxing, only guests receiving services should attend.
What is the cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations are charged 50% of the scheduled service; same-day cancellations and no-shows are charged 100%. Guests arriving 15+ minutes late may need to reschedule. Deposits are non-refundable but can be transferred with proper notice.

Do you cut men’s and kids’ hair?

Yes — precision cuts for women, men and children.

¿Hablas español?

¡Sí! Services are offered in English and Spanish.

What payment do you accept?

Visa, MasterCard and Discover.
